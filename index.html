望远镜智能控制实验室Telescope Intelligent Control Laboratory

望远镜智能控制实验室由望远镜技术专家崔向群院士和人工智能技术专家王怀清教授领衔，致力于融合人工智能方法与天文光学前沿技术，研究大口径光学望远镜的智能光学设计与加工、自主运行与健康管理、成像优化与数据处理等关键技术，服务于深空探测、光电技术与大口径望远镜建设等国家重大需求。

Led by Academician Cui Xiangqun, a renowned expert in telescope technology, and Prof. Wang Huaiqing, an expert in artificial intelligence, the Telescope Intelligent Control Laboratory integrates AI methods with frontier technologies in astronomical optics to develop key technologies for the intelligent optical design and manufacturing, autonomous operation and health management, and imaging optimization and data processing of large-aperture optical telescopes, serving major national needs in deep-space exploration, optoelectronic technology, and the construction of large-aperture telescopes.

光学望远镜中大模型与多智能体应用专题研讨会在南京召开（2025年6月） Symposium on Large Models and Multi-Agent Applications in Optical Telescopes (June 2025, Nanjing)
来自同济大学、南京大学、南京邮电大学、山东科技大学等单位的专家与青年学者参会，围绕 AI+天文光学应用前景展开研讨 Experts and young scholars from Tongji University, Nanjing University, NJUPT, SDUST, and more discussed the prospects of AI + astronomical optics

实验室围绕 智能光学设计与加工、自主运行与健康管理 与 成像优化与数据处理 三大方向开展研究，构建贯通设计、运行与数据处理的全生命周期智能化体系，推动望远镜从"人工值守"迈向"智能自主"。 Our research is organized around three directions — Intelligent Optical Design & Manufacturing, Autonomous Operation & Health Management, and Imaging Optimization & Data Processing — building a full-lifecycle intelligent system that advances telescopes from "manual operation" toward "intelligent autonomy".

🔬 智能光学设计与加工Intelligent Optical Design & Manufacturing 面向大口径望远镜与镜面磨制，研究设计约束与加工反馈耦合、中高频误差抑制与在位检测，提升光学设计与镜面加工的效率及一致性。 For large-aperture telescopes and mirror fabrication — coupling design constraints with fabrication feedback, mid/high-frequency error suppression, and on-machine inspection for more efficient, consistent optics and mirrors. 🛰️ 自主运行与健康管理Autonomous Operation & Health Management 面向 Dome A 与冷湖台站等极端台址，研究多模态感知、故障诊断及预测与自主决策，实现预测性维护、少人化运行与无人化值守。 Multimodal sensing, fault diagnosis and prognosis, and autonomous decision-making for extreme sites like Dome A and Lenghu — enabling predictive maintenance and unattended, reduced-manpower operation. 📡 成像优化与数据处理Imaging Optimization & Data Processing 应对大气扰动与卫星污染，研究湍流/像差检测与补偿、弱信号检测与提取及噪声智能抑制，实现复杂扰动下的高分辨成像与弱信号探测。 Turbulence/aberration detection and compensation, weak-signal detection and extraction, and intelligent noise suppression under atmospheric disturbance and satellite contamination — for high-resolution imaging and weak-signal detection.

实验室常年招收博士、硕士研究生与博士后，欢迎对天文、控制与智能技术感兴趣的同学加入我们。 We are always looking for passionate PhD students, master's students, and postdocs. If you are interested in astronomy, control, and intelligent technologies, welcome to our team!
