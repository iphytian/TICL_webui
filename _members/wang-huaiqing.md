<span class="zh">教授，博士生导师，望远镜智能控制实验室负责人。</span>

<span class="en">Professor, PhD supervisor, and head of the Telescope Intelligent Control Laboratory.</span>

<span class="zh">研究方向：望远镜高精度伺服控制、自抗扰控制理论及其在天文仪器中的应用。</span>

<span class="en">Research interests: high-precision servo control of telescopes, active disturbance rejection control (ADRC) theory, and their applications in astronomical instruments.</span>

<span class="zh">长期从事大口径望远镜伺服系统与智能控制技术研究，主持多项国家级科研项目，在望远镜跟踪控制、系统建模与辨识等方面取得系列研究成果。</span>

<span class="en">He has long been engaged in research on servo systems and intelligent control technologies for large-aperture telescopes, has led multiple national-level research projects, and has achieved a series of results in telescope tracking control, system modeling, and identification.</span>
