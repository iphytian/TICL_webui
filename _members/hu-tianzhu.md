<span class="zh">胡天柱，现任副研究员、硕士生导师。2015年本科毕业于东北师范大学物理学专业，2021年博士毕业于中国科学院大学天文技术与方法专业，同年加入中国科学院南京天文光学技术研究所。</span>

<span class="en">Dr. Hu Tianzhu is an Associate Researcher and master's supervisor. He received his B.S. in Physics from Northeast Normal University in 2015 and his Ph.D. in Astronomical Technology and Methods from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2021, and joined the Nanjing Institute of Astronomical Optics &amp; Technology, CAS in the same year.</span>

<span class="zh">主要从事人工智能与天文光学交叉研究，研究方向包括大型天文望远镜智能运行与健康管理、计算天文光学成像。</span>

<span class="en">His research focuses on the intersection of artificial intelligence and astronomical optics, including intelligent operation and health management of large astronomical telescopes, and computational astronomical imaging.</span>

<span class="zh">主持国家自然科学基金青年基金、江苏省自然科学基金青年基金，中国科学院特别研究资助项目，获中国科学院院长优秀奖，入选2025年江苏省青年科技人才托举工程。在国内外学术刊物上发表期刊论文10余篇，获授权国家发明专利2项。</span>

<span class="en">He leads the Young Scientists Fund of the NSFC and the Young Scientists Fund of the Jiangsu Provincial Natural Science Foundation, and was supported by the CAS Special Research Program. He received the CAS President Award and was selected for the 2025 Jiangsu Young Scientific and Technological Talent Support Program. He has published more than 10 papers in domestic and international journals and holds 2 granted national invention patents.</span>

<span class="zh">近年来代表性工作包括：（1）望远镜成像质量智能监测与诊断：建立了面向望远镜观测图像的实时异常监测方法（Hu et al. 2021, MNRAS），并进一步开展了基于知识图谱的异常原因诊断研究（Hu et al. 2023, MNRAS）；（2）大模型及多智能体的天文应用：开展LAMOST智能维护大模型构建、多智能体协作小样本故障诊断、视觉大模型异常检测等工作（Zhao et al. 2026, ESWA）；（3）卫星星座对天文观测影响评估：建立基于椭圆轨道模型的卫星影响评估方法，分析不同台站受卫星影响情况（Hu et al. 2024, A&amp;A）。</span>

<span class="en">His recent representative work includes: (1) Intelligent monitoring and diagnosis of telescope image quality — real-time anomaly monitoring of telescope observation images (Hu et al. 2021, MNRAS) and knowledge-graph-based diagnosis of anomaly causes (Hu et al. 2023, MNRAS); (2) Astronomical applications of large models and multi-agent systems — LAMOST intelligent maintenance large model, multi-agent collaborative few-shot fault diagnosis, and vision-large-model anomaly detection (Zhao et al. 2026, ESWA); (3) Assessment of satellite constellation impacts on astronomical observations — an elliptical-orbit-based evaluation method for satellite impacts at different observatories (Hu et al. 2024, A&amp;A).</span>
