<span class="zh">蔡静怡，现任工程师。2018 年本科毕业于南京信息工程大学电气工程及其自动化专业，2021 年硕士毕业于南京信息工程大学控制工程专业，2022 年加入中国科学院南京天文光学技术研究所。</span>

<span class="en">Cai Jingyi, currently an engineer. She received her B.S. degree in Electrical Engineering and Automation from Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology in 2018, and her M.S. degree in Control Engineering from the same university in 2021. She joined the Nanjing Institute of Astronomical Optics &amp; Technology, CAS, in 2022.</span>

<span class="zh">主要从事人工智能与天文装置设备控制交叉研究，研究方向包括大型望远镜智能化控制系统、大模型驱动的望远镜观测规划、天文装置设备智能可靠性管理。</span>

<span class="en">Her research focuses on the intersection of artificial intelligence and astronomical instrument control, including intelligent control systems for large telescopes, large-language-model-driven telescope observation planning, and intelligent reliability management of astronomical equipment.</span>

<span class="zh">作为核心成员参与国家自然科学基金面上项目，在国内学术刊物发表期刊论文多篇，提交国家发明专利多项。</span>

<span class="en">As a core member, she has participated in the General Program of the National Natural Science Foundation of China, published multiple papers in domestic academic journals, and filed several national invention patents.</span>

<span class="zh">近年来代表性工作包括：（1）大型天文光学望远镜智能化控制系统研究：融合深度学习与智能代理技术，面向望远镜可靠性管理与观测质量优化搭建智能控制系统，基于 LAMOST 历史数据完成仿真验证（蔡静怡等，2026，天文学报）；（2）基于大语言模型的望远镜观测规划技术研究：结合大语言模型、知识图谱与深度学习，实现望远镜观测任务解析与观测规划方案生成，为望远镜自主观测运行提供技术支撑（蔡静怡等，专利 CN202310955174.2）。</span>

<span class="en">Recent representative works include: (1) Research on intelligent control systems for large astronomical optical telescopes: integrating deep learning and intelligent agent technologies to build an intelligent control system oriented toward telescope reliability management and observation quality optimization, with simulation verification completed based on LAMOST historical data (Cai Jingyi et al., 2026, Acta Astronomica Sinica); (2) Research on telescope observation planning based on large language models: combining large language models, knowledge graphs, and deep learning to achieve telescope observation task parsing and observation planning generation, providing technical support for autonomous telescope observation operations (Cai Jingyi et al., Patent CN202310955174.2).</span>
