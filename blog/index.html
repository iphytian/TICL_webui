实验室牵头立项2026年江苏省前沿技术研发计划项目（基于多智能体的大口径光学镜面精密制造与检测技术研发） Lab Led 2026 Jiangsu Frontier Technology R&D Program Project (Multi-Agent-Based Precision Manufacturing and Inspection of Large-Aperture Optical Mirrors)
August 01, 2026 August 20, 2026
项目

2026年，实验室牵头申报的江苏省前沿技术研发计划项目"基于多智能体的大口径光学镜面精密制造与检测技术研发"成功立项。

In 2026, the Jiangsu Frontier Technology R&D Program project led by the laboratory was approved — precision manufacturing and inspection of large-aperture optical mirrors based on multi-agent technology.

In July 2025, the symposium on "Large Models and Multi-Agent Applications in Optical Telescopes" was held in Nanjing.

2020

实验室牵头立项天文领域首个智能化重点项目（大型天文光学望远镜控制系统智能化研究） Lab Led the First Intelligentization Key Project in Astronomy (Intelligent Control Systems for Large Astronomical Optical Telescopes)
January 01, 2020 August 20, 2026
项目

2020年，实验室牵头立项了天文领域第一个智能化重点项目——大型天文光学望远镜控制系统智能化研究。

In 2020, the laboratory led the first intelligentization key project in astronomy — intelligent control systems for large astronomical optical telescopes.
