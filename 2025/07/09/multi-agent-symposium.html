光学望远镜中大模型与多智能体应用专题研讨会在南京召开 Symposium on Large Models and Multi-Agent Applications in Optical Telescopes Held in Nanjing

July 09, 2025 August 17, 2026
学术交流

2025年7月，”光学望远镜中大模型与多智能体应用”专题研讨会在南京召开。研讨会围绕大模型与多智能体技术在光学望远镜中的应用开展深入研讨，来自国内多家科研院所和高校的专家学者参加会议。

In July 2025, the symposium on “Large Models and Multi-Agent Applications in Optical Telescopes” was held in Nanjing. Experts and scholars from research institutes and universities across China gathered to discuss the application of large models and multi-agent technologies in optical telescopes.

会议详情：中国科学院南京天文光学技术研究所新闻报道。

More details: News report by NIAOT, CAS.
