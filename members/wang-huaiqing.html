王怀清 教授 Wang Huaiqing 教授 Professor 望远镜智能控制实验室 Telescope Intelligent Control Laboratory
Email

王怀清博士，香港城市大学荣誉教授，中国科学院南京天文光学技术研究所客座研究员，中国科学院大学南京学院客座教授，人工智能技术专家。

Dr. Wang Huaiqing is an Honorary Professor at City University of Hong Kong, a Guest Researcher at the Nanjing Institute of Astronomical Optics & Technology, CAS, a Guest Professor at the Nanjing College, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, and an expert in artificial intelligence.

在人工智能研发领域拥有 40 余年经验，研究领域涵盖智能体与群体智能、智能决策支持、大数据智能、机器学习与大语言模型等，并致力于将人工智能方法应用于天文光学与望远镜智能化研究。

With more than 40 years of experience in AI research and development, his research covers intelligent agents and swarm intelligence, intelligent decision support, big-data intelligence, machine learning, and large language models, with a focus on applying AI methods to astronomical optics and intelligent telescopes.

在 SCI 期刊发表论文 98 篇（其中 70 余篇为 JCR 一区，首篇人工智能领域 SCI 论文发表于 1985 年），SCI 总引用 3000 余次、H 指数 27；Google Scholar 引用 10000 余次、H 指数 48。2014—2023 年连续十年入选爱思唯尔（Elsevier）中国高被引学者。

He has published 98 papers in SCI journals (over 70 in JCR Q1, with his first AI paper published in 1985), with more than 3,000 SCI citations and an H-index of 27; Google Scholar citations exceed 10,000 with an H-index of 48. He was recognized as a Highly Cited Chinese Researcher by Elsevier for ten consecutive years from 2014 to 2023.

曾获 2019 年度中国自动化学会自然科学奖一等奖、2016 年度中国电子学会自然科学奖一等奖、2012 年度上海市自然科学奖二等奖。

He received the First Prize of the CAA Natural Science Award (2019), the First Prize of the CIE Natural Science Award (2016), and the Second Prize of the Shanghai Natural Science Award (2012).

主持过多项国家级科研与人工智能研发项目，包括国家自然科学基金重点项目”大型天文光学望远镜控制系统智能化研究”（2020—2024），以及智能金融监控、智能决策支持、智能教育、智能核电站监控等系列项目。

He has led numerous national-level research and AI development projects, including the NSFC Key Program “Intelligent Control Systems for Large Astronomical Optical Telescopes” (2020–2024), as well as projects on intelligent financial monitoring, intelligent decision support, intelligent education, and intelligent nuclear power plant monitoring.
