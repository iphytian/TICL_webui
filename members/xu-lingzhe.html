徐灵哲 副研究员 Xu Lingzhe 副研究员 Associate Researcher 望远镜智能控制实验室 Telescope Intelligent Control Laboratory
Email

徐灵哲，副研究员，硕士生导师，天文学会信息委员会首届成员。1997年本科毕业于河海大学计算机应用专业，2004年硕士毕业于南京大学计算机应用工程专业，2008年在中国科学院南京天文光学技术研究所获得天体物理博士学位。2002年至今在中国科学院南京天文光学技术研究所工作。

Xu Lingzhe is an Associate Researcher and master’s supervisor at the Nanjing Institute of Astronomical Optics & Technology (NIAOT), CAS, and one of the founding members of the Information Committee of the Chinese Astronomical Society. He received his B.S. in Computer Application from Hohai University in 1997, his M.Eng. in Computer Application Engineering from Nanjing University in 2004, and his Ph.D. in Astrophysics from NIAOT, CAS in 2008. He has been working at NIAOT since 2002.

主要从事天文望远镜控制系统、观测控制系统与远程控制系统的软件设计与研发。

His research focuses on the software design and development of telescope control systems, observation control systems, and remote control systems for astronomical telescopes.

多年来先后参与多个望远镜相关控制系统的软件设计，是LAMOST望远镜控制系统软件、南极AST3望远镜远程通讯软件、俄罗斯2.5米望远镜观测控制系统软件等系统的主要设计者和开发者。2012年，作为第二十九次中国南极科学考察队内陆队成员抵达南极冰盖最高点 Dome A，成功完成AST3望远镜的现场维护。

Over the years he has participated in the software design of control systems for several telescopes, serving as a main designer and developer of the LAMOST telescope control system software, the remote communication software of the Antarctic AST3 telescope, and the observation control system software of the Russian 2.5 m telescope. In 2012, as a member of the inland team of the 29th Chinese National Antarctic Research Expedition, he reached Dome A, the highest point of the Antarctic ice sheet, and successfully carried out on-site maintenance of the AST3 telescope.

近年来从事人工智能与望远镜控制系统相结合的交叉研究，获得深度学习与开发应用高级人才证书，是国家自然科学基金联合基金重点项目”大型天文光学望远镜控制系统智能化研究”的核心骨干成员。

In recent years he has worked at the intersection of artificial intelligence and telescope control systems. He holds the Senior Talent Certificate in Deep Learning and Development Applications and is a core member of the NSFC Joint Fund Key Program “Research on the Intelligentization of Control Systems for Large Astronomical Optical Telescopes”.

以第一作者身份发表学术论文10余篇，获授权国家发明专利2项。

He has published more than 10 academic papers as first author and holds 2 granted national invention patents.
