招生信息 Admissions

实验室常年招收博士、硕士研究生与博士后：

The laboratory recruits PhD students, master’s students, and postdocs throughout the year:

博士研究生PhD Students

每年招收 1–2 名，要求控制理论与控制工程、自动化、机械电子、计算机等相关专业，具有扎实的数学与控制理论基础。

1–2 PhD students per year in control theory & engineering, automation, mechatronics, computer science, or related fields, with solid foundations in mathematics and control theory.

硕士研究生Master's Students

每年招收 3–4 名，要求自动化、电子信息、计算机、光电等相关专业，鼓励参与竞赛与科创项目，具有编程实践经验者优先。

3–4 master's students per year in automation, electronic information, computer science, optoelectronics, or related fields. Competition/project experience and programming skills are preferred.

博士后Postdocs

常年招聘，待遇从优，研究方向与实验室重点方向契合，提供充足的科研经费与实验平台支持。

Postdoctoral positions are open year-round with competitive packages for research aligned with our focus areas, supported by adequate funding and experimental platforms.
