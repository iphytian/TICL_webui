<span class="zh">2020年，实验室牵头立项了天文领域第一个智能化重点项目——"大型天文光学望远镜控制系统智能化研究"。</span>

<span class="en">In 2020, the laboratory led the approval of the first intelligentization key project in the astronomical field — "Intelligent Control Systems for Large Astronomical Optical Telescopes".</span>

<span class="zh">项目面向大口径天文光学望远镜控制系统智能化的重大需求，围绕智能控制理论与方法、控制系统智能化关键技术开展系统研究，为实验室在望远镜智能化研究方向的发展奠定了坚实基础。</span>

<span class="en">Aiming at the intelligent control of large-aperture astronomical optical telescopes, the project conducted systematic research on intelligent control theory and key intelligent control technologies, laying a solid foundation for the laboratory's development in intelligent telescope research.</span>
