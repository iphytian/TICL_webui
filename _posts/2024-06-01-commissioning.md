<span class="zh">团队成员赴合作天文台站完成了望远镜控制系统的安装与调试工作，系统各项指标达到设计要求。</span>

<span class="en">The team completed the installation and commissioning of a telescope control system at a partner observatory, with all indicators meeting the design requirements.</span>

<span class="zh">本次联调测试验证了实验室自主研发的控制系统在实际观测环境中的跟踪精度与稳定性，为后续的长时间观测任务奠定了坚实基础。</span>

<span class="en">The commissioning verified the tracking accuracy and stability of the laboratory's self-developed control system in real observation environments, laying a solid foundation for subsequent long-term observation missions.</span>
