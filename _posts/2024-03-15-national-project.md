<span class="zh">实验室牵头申报的"智能望远镜关键技术"相关课题成功获批，项目研究周期为三年。</span>

<span class="en">The project on "key technologies for intelligent telescopes" led by the laboratory has been approved, with a research period of three years.</span>

<span class="zh">该课题将围绕望远镜智能控制的核心技术问题开展攻关，为提升我国大型望远镜设备的自动化与智能化水平提供关键技术支撑。</span>

<span class="en">The project will tackle core technical problems in intelligent telescope control, providing key technological support for improving the automation and intelligence of large telescope facilities in China.</span>
