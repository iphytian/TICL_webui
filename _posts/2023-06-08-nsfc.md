<span class="zh">实验室申报的国家自然科学基金面上项目成功获批，研究经费将用于望远镜高精度跟踪控制相关研究。</span>

<span class="en">The National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) General Program applied for by the laboratory has been approved, and the funding will support research on high-precision tracking control of telescopes.</span>

<span class="zh">该项目将重点解决复杂观测环境下望远镜跟踪控制的鲁棒性与自适应问题，进一步提升实验室在该方向的研究水平。</span>

<span class="en">The project will focus on the robustness and adaptability of telescope tracking control under complex observation conditions, further enhancing the laboratory's research in this direction.</span>
