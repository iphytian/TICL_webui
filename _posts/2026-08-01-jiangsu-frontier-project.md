<span class="zh">2026年，实验室牵头申报的江苏省前沿技术研发计划项目"基于多智能体的大口径光学镜面精密制造与检测技术研发"成功立项。</span>

<span class="en">In 2026, the Jiangsu Frontier Technology R&D Program project "Precision Manufacturing and Inspection of Large-Aperture Optical Mirrors Based on Multi-Agent Technology", led by the laboratory, was successfully approved.</span>

<span class="zh">项目将围绕大口径光学镜面精密制造与检测的智能化需求，研究多智能体协同的关键技术与装备，支撑我国大口径光学系统研制能力的提升。</span>

<span class="en">The project will address the intelligent manufacturing and inspection of large-aperture optical mirrors through multi-agent collaboration, supporting China's capability in developing large-aperture optical systems.</span>
