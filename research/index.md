# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-microscope" %}<span class="zh">研究方向</span> <span class="en">Research</span>

<span class="zh">实验室面向大型天文望远镜全生命周期智能化建设的重大需求，构建贯通 <strong>智能光学设计与加工、自主运行与健康管理、成像优化与数据处理</strong> 的全生命周期智能化体系，推动望远镜从"人工值守"迈向"智能自主"。</span>

<span class="en">The laboratory builds a full-lifecycle intelligent system spanning <strong>intelligent optical design and manufacturing, autonomous operation and health management, and imaging optimization and data processing</strong> — advancing large telescopes from "manual operation" toward "intelligent autonomy".</span>

  <p class="en">For extreme sites such as Dome A and the Lenghu Observatory, we study multimodal sensing, fault diagnosis and prognosis, and autonomous decision-making, building an integrated "sense–diagnose–predict–decide" system so telescopes can run safely and reliably with minimal human intervention.</p>

<div class="grid-card">
