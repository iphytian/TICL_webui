# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-microscope" %}<span class="zh">研究方向</span> <span class="en">Research</span>

<span class="zh">实验室面向大型天文望远镜智能化运维与自主观测的重大需求，围绕 <strong>望远镜健康管理、望远镜自主运行、AI+天文光学</strong> 三大方向开展系统性研究，推动望远镜从"人工值守"迈向"智能自主"。</span>

<span class="en">The laboratory conducts systematic research around three major directions — telescope health management, autonomous telescope operation, and AI + astronomical optics — to advance large telescopes from "manual operation" toward "intelligent autonomy".</span>

{% include section.html %}

## <span class="zh">研究领域</span> <span class="en">Research Areas</span>

{% capture content %}

<div class="grid-card">
  <div class="grid-card-title"><span class="zh">🔭 望远镜健康管理</span><span class="en">Telescope Health Management</span></div>
  <p class="zh">面向望远镜长期稳定运行需求，研究关键部件与系统的状态监测、故障诊断与预测、剩余寿命评估与维护决策技术，构建"感知—诊断—预测—决策"一体化体系，推动望远镜从"事后维修"向"预测性维护"转变，保障大型设备安全可靠运行。</p>
  <p class="en">Focusing on long-term reliable operation, we study condition monitoring, fault diagnosis and prognosis, remaining useful life assessment, and maintenance decision-making, building an integrated "sense–diagnose–predict–decide" system that shifts telescopes from corrective maintenance to predictive maintenance.</p>
</div>

<div class="grid-card">
  <div class="grid-card-title"><span class="zh">🤖 望远镜自主运行</span><span class="en">Autonomous Telescope Operation</span></div>
  <p class="zh">面向无人值守与自主观测需求，研究自主导星跟踪、智能观测调度、任务自动规划与故障自愈等技术，构建"感知—决策—执行"闭环的自主运行体系，实现望远镜在复杂环境下长时间、高效率、少干预地自主运行。</p>
  <p class="en">Focusing on unattended and autonomous observation, we study auto-guiding, intelligent observation scheduling, autonomous mission planning, and fault self-recovery, building a closed "perceive–decide–act" loop for long-duration, high-efficiency operation with minimal human intervention.</p>
</div>

<div class="grid-card">
  <div class="grid-card-title"><span class="zh">✨ AI+天文光学</span><span class="en">AI + Astronomical Optics</span></div>
  <p class="zh">将人工智能与天文光学深度融合，研究智能图像处理、目标检测与识别、光学像质智能优化与智能光学系统设计等技术，以数据驱动与物理模型相结合的方式，提升望远镜成像质量与科学产出能力。</p>
  <p class="en">We combine artificial intelligence with astronomical optics, studying intelligent image processing, object detection and recognition, image-quality optimization, and smart optical system design, blending data-driven and physics-based methods to improve imaging quality and scientific output.</p>
</div>
