研究方向 Research

实验室面向大型天文望远镜全生命周期智能化建设的重大需求，构建贯通 智能光学设计与加工、自主运行与健康管理、成像优化与数据处理 的全生命周期智能化体系，推动望远镜从”人工值守”迈向”智能自主”。

The laboratory builds a full-lifecycle intelligent system spanning intelligent optical design and manufacturing, autonomous operation and health management, and imaging optimization and data processing — advancing large telescopes from “manual operation” toward “intelligent autonomy”.

研究领域 Research Areas

🔬 智能光学设计与加工Intelligent Optical Design & Manufacturing

关键技术Key Technologies

面向大口径望远镜与大口径镜面磨制，研究设计约束与加工反馈的双向耦合、中高频误差抑制与在位检测，打通"设计—加工—检测"闭环，实现光学设计与镜面加工的高效率与高一致性。

For large-aperture telescopes and large-aperture mirror fabrication, we study the bidirectional coupling of design constraints and fabrication feedback, mid/high-frequency error suppression, and on-machine inspection, closing the design–fabrication–testing loop to make optical design and mirror fabrication more efficient and consistent.

  • 设计约束 ↔ 加工反馈Design constraints ↔ fabrication feedback
  • 中高频误差抑制Mid/high-frequency error suppression
  • 在位检测On-machine / in-situ inspection

实现Goal

提升光学设计与镜面加工的效率及一致性。

Improve the efficiency and consistency of optical design and mirror fabrication.

🛰️ 自主运行与健康管理Autonomous Operation & Health Management

关键技术Key Technologies

面向 Dome A、冷湖台站等极端台址，研究多模态感知、故障诊断及预测与自主决策技术，构建"感知—诊断—预测—决策"一体化体系，保障望远镜长期安全可靠运行，实现少人化乃至无人化值守。

For extreme sites such as Dome A and the Lenghu Observatory, we study multimodal sensing, fault diagnosis and prognosis, and autonomous decision-making, building an integrated "sense–diagnose–predict–decide" system so telescopes can run safely and reliably with minimal human intervention.

  • 多模态感知Multimodal sensing
  • 故障诊断及预测Fault diagnosis and prognosis
  • 自主决策Autonomous decision-making

实现Goal

预测性维护、少人化运行与无人化值守。

Predictive maintenance, reduced-manpower operation, and unattended duty.

📡 成像优化与数据处理Imaging Optimization & Data Processing

关键技术Key Technologies

面向大气扰动与卫星污染等复杂成像环境，研究湍流/像差检测与补偿、弱信号检测与提取及噪声智能抑制，以数据驱动与物理模型相结合的方式，提升望远镜成像质量与科学产出能力。

Under complex imaging conditions such as atmospheric turbulence and satellite contamination, we study turbulence/aberration detection and compensation, weak-signal detection and extraction, and intelligent noise suppression, blending data-driven and physics-based methods to improve imaging quality and scientific output.

  • 湍流/像差检测与补偿Turbulence/aberration detection & compensation
  • 弱信号检测与提取Weak-signal detection & extraction
  • 噪声智能抑制Intelligent noise suppression

实现Goal

复杂扰动下的高分辨成像与弱信号探测。

High-resolution imaging and weak-signal detection under complex disturbance.
