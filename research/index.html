研究方向 Research

实验室面向大型天文望远镜智能化运维与自主观测的重大需求，围绕 望远镜健康管理、望远镜自主运行、AI+天文光学 三大方向开展系统性研究，推动望远镜从”人工值守”迈向”智能自主”。

The laboratory conducts systematic research around three major directions — telescope health management, autonomous telescope operation, and AI + astronomical optics — to advance large telescopes from “manual operation” toward “intelligent autonomy”.

研究领域 Research Areas

🔭 望远镜健康管理Telescope Health Management

面向望远镜长期稳定运行需求，研究关键部件与系统的状态监测、故障诊断与预测、剩余寿命评估与维护决策技术，构建"感知—诊断—预测—决策"一体化体系，推动望远镜从"事后维修"向"预测性维护"转变，保障大型设备安全可靠运行。

Focusing on long-term reliable operation, we study condition monitoring, fault diagnosis and prognosis, remaining useful life assessment, and maintenance decision-making, building an integrated "sense–diagnose–predict–decide" system that shifts telescopes from corrective maintenance to predictive maintenance.

🤖 望远镜自主运行Autonomous Telescope Operation

面向无人值守与自主观测需求，研究自主导星跟踪、智能观测调度、任务自动规划与故障自愈等技术，构建"感知—决策—执行"闭环的自主运行体系，实现望远镜在复杂环境下长时间、高效率、少干预地自主运行。

Focusing on unattended and autonomous observation, we study auto-guiding, intelligent observation scheduling, autonomous mission planning, and fault self-recovery, building a closed "perceive–decide–act" loop for long-duration, high-efficiency operation with minimal human intervention.

✨ AI+天文光学AI + Astronomical Optics

将人工智能与天文光学深度融合，研究智能图像处理、目标检测与识别、光学像质智能优化与智能光学系统设计等技术，以数据驱动与物理模型相结合的方式，提升望远镜成像质量与科学产出能力。

We combine artificial intelligence with astronomical optics, studying intelligent image processing, object detection and recognition, image-quality optimization, and smart optical system design, blending data-driven and physics-based methods to improve imaging quality and scientific output.
